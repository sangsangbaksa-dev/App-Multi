Questions

What if I clear my browser data?

The stamps go with it. Use Backup now and then, and restore from the file. Installing the page to your home screen does not change this.

Does the balance link show my other students?

No. The link carries one student's name, lesson dates and count, packed into the address itself. Anyone with the link can read it, so share it only with that student.

Does it handle payments or reminders?

No. It counts lessons and money already paid. It sends nothing for you.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the page in your phone browser and add it to the home screen. It works offline once loaded.
