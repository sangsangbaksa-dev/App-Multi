# Stampbook

A stamp card for prepaid lessons (tutors, coaches, instructors). Static site, no server, data in localStorage. 5 languages (en, ko, ja, zh, es). Free: 3 active packs. Pro: unlimited packs, balance links, CSV.

- `index.html` landing, `app/` the app, `privacy.html`
- Tabs: Packs, Calendar (month view, tap a day to see who was stamped) and Insights (6-month trend, busiest weekdays, per-student attendance, students gone quiet). Each pack can also have a weekly schedule (days + time): a Today list with one-tap stamping, planned days in the calendar, a `.ics` export, and a backup nudge after 14 days. Packs can be marked unpaid (shown in Insights with the amount awaiting), Insights also forecasts the next 30 days and searches lesson notes. Each pack can copy a month report for the student, and the new-pack form offers your most-used terms as one-tap presets. A planned lesson can be skipped (holiday) and restored from the Today list or calendar. Restoring a backup can merge into current data (stamps are unioned by id) instead of replacing it. Insights can export per-student stats as CSV (Pro). Make-up lessons: a lesson cancelled in time (Excused) is owed back; a one-tap Make-up lesson button pays it back and Insights lists who is owed. Packs that will not finish before they expire (based on the recent pace) get a warning on the card and in Insights. Each pack can copy a receipt, and deleting a pack is undoable. Lesson tools: "Stamp all" stamps every planned lesson of the day in one tap, "Copy list" copies today's lessons with times, and a stamp's date can be moved from the lesson log (`moveStamp`, `stampPlanned`, `dayPlanText` in `app/core.js`). More: "Copy tomorrow" and "Copy this week" copy the plan, each card shows its next lesson and a progress bar, "Duplicate" makes a new pack with the same terms and schedule, "Skip dates" skips a date range, Insights warns about lessons stamped twice, the pack list can sort by name, new-pack names are suggested from existing students, and "Export this pack" saves one pack as a backup file. Logic is in `app/core.js` (`monthGrid`, `dayMap`, `monthlyTrend`, `studentStats`, `quiet`, `overview`).
