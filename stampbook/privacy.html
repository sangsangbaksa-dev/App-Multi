Privacy

Stampbook runs entirely in your browser. Student names, dates and prices are saved in this browser's local storage and are never sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics and no cookies.

A balance link stores one student's name, lesson dates and lesson count inside the address after the # sign. That part of an address is not sent to servers when the page loads, but anyone who has the link can read it, and it may be kept by the chat app you paste it into. Share it only with that student.

Clearing your browser data deletes your stamps. Use Backup to keep a file copy.
