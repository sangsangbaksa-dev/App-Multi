Keep a log nobody can say you made up.

Marginlog records each incident with a timestamp, seals it into a tamper-evident chain, spots the pattern, and builds the report you give a landlord, HR, mediator or officer. Free, offline, no account.

Open Marginlog

How it works

  1. Pick the kind of problem and name the case.
  2. Log incidents in under 20 seconds, or time one live.
  3. Hand over the report when you need it.

What is inside

  • Sealed entries

    Each entry is fingerprinted with the one before it. Edit history and the report shows the chain broke.

  • Happening-now timer

    Tap once while it is happening. Duration and start time fill in for you.

  • Pattern finder

    Weekday-by-hour heat map, 8-week trend, quiet-hour breaches, longest calm stretch.

  • Evidence strength

    Each entry gets a score and tells you what to add: duration, witness, photo, timeliness.

  • Escalation ladder

    Talk, written notice, complaint, mediation, legal. Answer deadlines warn you when ignored.

  • Report in one tap

    Copy, print to PDF, Markdown or CSV, with photos and a seal check at the bottom.

Private by design

Everything is stored in your browser on this device. No account, no tracking, no upload. Download a backup file whenever you like.

A personal record, not legal advice. The seal shows the log was not rewritten; it is not a notarised timestamp.
