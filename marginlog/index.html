Honest answers

Will this hold up as evidence?

It can help you remember accurately and show a clear, consistent record. Whether any record is accepted depends on the country and the body deciding. It is not legal advice.

What does sealing prove?

That the entries were not quietly changed or removed after they were written. It is not a notarised timestamp, so it cannot prove when an entry was made.

Where is my data?

Only in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Clearing site data erases it, so download a backup now and then.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No ads, no sign-up.
