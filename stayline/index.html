Questions

Is the count legally binding?

No. It is an estimate from the dates you enter. Border officers and official government sources decide. Check your passport stamps and keep your tickets.

How are days counted?

The first and last day inside each count as full days. Overlapping trips count each day once.

Where is my data?

In this browser only (local storage). Clear site data and it is gone, so use Backup now and then.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the first visit. You can add it to your home screen.
