## Product Hunt (EN)
**Name:** Stayline
**Tagline (60 chars max):** Count your Schengen days. No account, no GPS.
**Description:** Type the dates you were in, see the days you have left, and plan the next trip: how long you can stay if you arrive on a date, or the earliest arrival for N days. Schengen 90/180, 183-day tax rule or your own limit. Everything stays in your browser. Free for one counter; Pro is a one-time payment.
**First comment:** I kept redoing the 90/180 maths in a spreadsheet and every tracker app wanted my location or a login. So this one only takes dates, runs in the browser and works offline. The counting follows the European Commission's calculator manual (I reproduced its worked examples as tests), but it is an estimate: officers and official sources decide. Feedback welcome, especially on rules for countries other than Schengen.
