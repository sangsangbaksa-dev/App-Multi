# Why Stayline (research notes, 2026-10-30)

Searched App Store listings via web search (titles only; I could not open pricing or review pages, so prices, ratings and download counts are **unconfirmed**).

- Day-count and Schengen 90/180 apps: many (e.g. "Schengen Calculator 90/180", "Schengen Calculator & Planner", "Schengen 90/180 Calculator", "BorderWise"). Country/tax-day trackers: also many ("Country Days Tracker", "Immio", "Pebbles", "WanderDays", ...). So demand exists, but the category is **not empty**.
- Other candidates checked and rejected as equally or more crowded: reseller profit trackers (ResellStats, FlipperHelper, ...), tip-pool calculators (Tip Out, Simple Tip Pool, ...), freelancer tax set-aside trackers (StashTax, ...). Korean gift-money ledgers (마음장부 etc.) exist too and monetise weakly.
- Chosen because the rule is exact and testable, the pain is recurring and costs real money, a static no-server app can serve it, and one-time payment is plausible. Gap claimed (unverified at scale): most listings are native iOS apps; this is a no-install, no-login, no-GPS web app with forward planning, in 5 languages, one payment.
- Not claimed: that it will earn money. Demand for a *paid* web version is untested.
