Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

The trips you enter are stored in this browser on your device (local storage). They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Stayline has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and never asks for your location.

Clearing the site data of your browser deletes your trips. Use Backup in the app to keep a copy.

When you activate a Pro licence key, the key is sent to our server and on to our payment provider only to check that it is valid. Nothing else is sent. Payments are handled by that provider under its own privacy policy.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses for a short time.

Questions: support@example.com.
