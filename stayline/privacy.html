Privacy

Stayline has no server that receives your data. The trips you enter are stored in your browser's local storage on this device and are never sent anywhere. We do not use accounts, cookies, analytics, or location access.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes your trips. Use Backup to save a file.

The Pro purchase, once enabled, is handled by an outside payment provider that has its own privacy policy. Contact: support@example.com (placeholder, to be replaced before launch).

Stayline gives estimates only and is not legal or immigration advice.
